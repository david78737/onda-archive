The problem every SMB has

Every small business has institutional knowledge trapped inside the heads of one or two people. When those people are training someone new, onboarding a client, or walking through a process, it happens live — and then it's gone.

The businesses that try to capture it end up with:

What they have today

  • Screen recordings that nobody watches because there's no index or search
  • PDFs and Word docs that go stale the moment anything changes
  • YouTube "private" videos that are clunky to share and impossible to navigate
  • No way for employees to jump to the exact moment they need

An IT firm visits these clients regularly. They see this problem at every shop. And they're already trusted as the people who fix things that don't work.

What ONDA makes possible

The offer

The IT professional becomes a Curator Curator on ONDA. For each client, they set up a private station — a searchable, indexed archive of that business's own instructional content. The client records a screen walkthrough, a process explanation, a training video. The IT firm uploads it. Within minutes it's a searchable .onda file with full karaoke-playback transcription, ready to share with anyone in the company.

The IT firm isn't selling software. They're selling a managed knowledge service. ONDA is the engine running underneath it.

How it works — step by step

  1. 1
    IT firm sets up a Curator account
    One $19.99/mo Curator subscription. One login. Manages all client stations from a single dashboard.
  2. 2
    Creates a station for each client
    Each business gets their own private station — their content, indexed under their name, visible only to people they choose.
  3. 3
    Client records their content
    A shop owner walks through a repair process on video. A manager records an onboarding session. A trainer demos software. MP4 or WAV — whatever they already have.
  4. 4
    Client sends the file to the IT firm
    No special app. No upload portal for the client. They just share the file the way they already share files — email, Drive, WeTransfer, whatever.
  5. 5
    IT firm runs bulk upload
    Up to 5 files per block at $4.99. The Whisper pipeline transcribes, indexes, and packages each file into a searchable .onda. Processing completes in minutes.
  6. 6
    .onda files land in the client's station archive
    Full transcript. Karaoke playback. Word-level search. The client's employees can now jump to the exact moment they need, every time.
  7. 7
    IT firm shares access with the client's team
    Listeners Listener download the free ONDA Replay app and receive the .onda files. Nothing to configure. Nothing to maintain.
  8. 8
    Station grows over time
    Every new process, every new hire, every update — another upload block, another set of searchable files. The knowledge base compounds.
