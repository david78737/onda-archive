ONDA Fuel — available at every tier

Need more transcription hours than your role includes? Buy Fuel at any time. $5.99 for 5 hours — hours never expire and stack with your monthly allowance. Listeners, Contributors, and Curators can all add Fuel whenever they need it.

★

The Curator role is a different category entirely.

Listener and Contributor are personal tools — your recordings, on your device, for your use. A Curator station is a public presence. It's how a Toastmasters chapter, a ProductCamp, or an individual expert builds a permanent, searchable archive that anyone can discover on ONDA Nile. One station can serve an entire organization.
