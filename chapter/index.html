This is the same "Record it in the app" button up top — it opens ONDA Replay so you can record your voice directly. Recording your own voice is the heart of ONDA Replay, so here's the process in a few short stages — no need to do it all in one sitting.

Stage 1 — Record a first take~2–5 min

Open the app and just start talking. Feeling awkward speaking into a mic? Start small — a sentence or two at a time. Don't worry about ums, errs, or stumbles — say it rough, then stop.

Stage 2 — Read the clean version back~3–5 min, repeat as needed

Stopping transcribes your rough take into plain text — no ums, no errs, just the words. Open a new recording and read that text back out loud. That take comes out clean — keep it and delete the rough one. Once you've got two or three sentences, that's already a paragraph. You can re-record it all together as one smooth take, but you don't have to.

Stage 3 — Save and send~1 min

When you're happy with it, stop, confirm it's saved, and send it with "Send with a Message." It lands right here on the station.

What matters most isn't a flawless take — it's that it sounds like you. Authentic and natural, like sharing something with a friend across the table.

On the story form, tap "Upload a photo" — that opens your phone's own picker so you can browse straight to the photo, no link or typing needed. PNG or JPG work best.

On iPhone or iPad

Tapping "Upload a photo" opens your Photos library right there — scroll or search for the picture and tap it. If it was taken on this phone it may be HEIC, which won't display here; if the upload is rejected, open the photo in Photos, tap Share → Save to Files, and upload that JPG copy instead.

On Android

Tapping "Upload a photo" opens your phone's own photo picker — browse to the picture in Gallery, Photos, or Recent and tap it. Most Android photos are already JPG.
